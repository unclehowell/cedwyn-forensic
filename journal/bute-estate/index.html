6 November 2026Journal entryCourtesy search: no results; enquiries paused to January 2027ref Mount Stuart Trust, Bute Collection (Bute Estate Glamorgan records, 1667 and 1875-1880; Mountjoy Ltd)

Email of 6 November 2026, 16:53 UTC, from the Collections Team of the Bute Collection at Mount Stuart, answering the requester's emails of 15, 19, 29 and 31 October 2026 (a research-visit request and a preservation and records request about Mountjoy Ltd).

Assessment (our reading): A courteous answer from a private archive with no duty to reply: a free short search found nothing, with plain caveats that half the archive and the whole Mountjoy collection are uncatalogued. It neither confirms nor excludes the records sought.

What they did

  • Explained that enquiries are paused, to be reviewed in January 2027, for staffing reasons, and that research spaces are booked until Christmas.
  • Ran a free 30-minute basic search of the holdings as a courtesy and reported no results for the documents asked about.
  • Stated that the archive is about 50% catalogued and draft lists are not available externally, and that the Mountjoy Ltd collection is privately owned and uncatalogued.
  • Stated that under data protection law it cannot say whether anyone has asked to inspect, borrow or receive documents from that collection.

What they did not do

  • No search terms, series or lists searched are named.
  • The preservation request is not answered directly.

Fair note

  • The Mount Stuart Trust is a registered Scottish charity, not a public authority, so neither the Freedom of Information Act nor the Freedom of Information (Scotland) Act applies; the search was given free while enquiries are closed.
  • A 30-minute search of a half-catalogued archive cannot rule records in or out, and the reply does not present it as doing so.
  • Declining to name other researchers is a usual privacy position for a private archive.

Open questions

  • Will the Mountjoy Ltd collection be listed when enquiries reopen in 2027, and can a visit be booked then?
