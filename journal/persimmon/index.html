4 November 2026Journal entryBasis questioned; rights reservedref Persimmon Litigation (titles WA231076 / WA240304)

First substantive reply from Persimmon (Litigation team, Persimmon Homes Limited, 10:13 UTC, 4 November 2026) to the requester's emails of 27 September and 31 October 2026. Those emails asked Persimmon to preserve, and voluntarily disclose, non-privileged records about Church View Close / Great House Farm, Llandough (titles WA231076 and WA240304): any epitome, abstract or deeds bundle supplied on the purchase from BP Properties Ltd by Ideal Homes Wales (planning papers record a transfer of 3 December 1993), copies of a Mountjoy Limited document, a 1926 conveyance to the Corporation of Cardiff and a conveyance of 25 December 1938 to Western Ground Rents Ltd, the solicitors who acted, where the pre-registration deeds are, and whether anyone has sought them. A preservation hold confirmation was asked for by 5 November and a reply by 12 November 2026.

Assessment (our reading): A prompt, formal holding position rather than an answer. Persimmon questions the basis for voluntary disclosure, which it is entitled to do as a private company outside proceedings, and doubts that papers up to 100 years old survive. It does not address the more recent 1993 purchase papers, the solicitors, the correct entity, or whether a preservation hold is in place. Those remain the open points; a voluntary request has no statutory deadline behind it.

What they did

  • Replied 38 days after the first email of 27 September 2026 and four days after the follow-up of 31 October 2026, before both dates the requester gave.
  • Referred to both emails and to the request for voluntary or pre-action disclosure of non-privileged material.
  • Stated that the basis on which the requester would be entitled to any of the documents is not clear to Persimmon.
  • Stated that some of the documents appear to be up to 100 years old and that it is therefore unlikely Persimmon retains copies.
  • Reserved Persimmon's rights in full.

What they did not do

  • Did not state whether a preservation hold has been placed, the point asked for by 5 November 2026.
  • Did not state whether the deeds bundle, epitome or abstract from the 1993 purchase is held; the 'up to 100 years old' point concerns the 1922 to 1938 documents, not papers from a purchase about 33 years ago.
  • Did not name the solicitors who acted on the purchase or the later sales, or say where the pre-registration deeds now are.
  • Did not confirm whether Persimmon Homes (Wales) Limited (00461464), named in the 31 October email, is the right entity; the reply is from Persimmon Homes Limited.
  • Did not answer whether anyone has asked for, copied or removed the papers.

Fair note

  • Persimmon is a private company. The Freedom of Information Act 2000 and the EIR do not apply to it, and outside proceedings it has no general duty to disclose documents to a third party. Pre-action disclosure is ordinarily a matter for an application to the court under CPR 31.16, where the applicant must show the parties are likely to be parties to proceedings; a request for voluntary disclosure is at the company's discretion. Asking on what basis a requester rests the request is a normal response from a litigation team.
  • The reply is short, prompt and courteous, and comes from a named company solicitor, which is better than silence or an automatic acknowledgement.
  • Doubting that 1920s and 1930s papers survive with a developer is reasonable on its face. The request also asked about papers from the 1993 purchase and a preservation hold; the reply does not separate those from the older documents.
  • A reservation of rights is a standard litigation formula and does not by itself indicate a position on the facts.

Open questions

  • Has a preservation hold been placed on any records about titles WA231076 and WA240304, and if so since when?
  • Does any Persimmon or Ideal Homes company, or its former solicitors, hold the deeds bundle or abstract from the 1993 purchase from BP Properties Ltd?
  • Which Persimmon entity is the correct party for the Ideal Homes Wales interest in this land?
  • Which solicitors acted on the 1993 purchase?
