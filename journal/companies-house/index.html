3 November 2026Journal entryFiles destroyed; imaged records onlyref COH2382411X (Archive Searches)

Two replies from the Companies House Archive Searches team (10:27 and 10:34 UTC, 3 November 2026), one for each company named in the requester's email of 2 November 2026: Western Ground Rents Limited (00342092) and Mountjoy Limited (00179977). The requester's email asked, for each company, whether any paper file, microfiche, image or index entry is held, whether the file was transferred to The National Archives or destroyed (with date, reference and authority), whether a transfer or destruction record is held, whether a preservation hold is in place, and asked for the matter to go to the FOI team if the Freedom of Information Act 2000 applies.

Assessment (our reading): Prompt, clear and courteous answer on the core point: neither company file went to The National Archives, and paper and microfiche were destroyed, leaving only imaged filings. On the disposal dates Companies House gave on 2 November, destruction predates the preservation requests. The replies do not give dates, the authority, any destruction record, a yes or no on the preservation hold, or an FOI reference, and they leave the conveyance questions unanswered. Those are the open points.

What they did

  • Replied one working day after the requester's email of 2 November 2026 (22:11 UTC), well inside the 20 working days of FOIA section 10.
  • Confirmed the company details: Western Ground Rents Limited, 00342092, incorporated 2 July 1938, dissolved 22 August 1997; Mountjoy Limited, 00179977, incorporated 27 February 1922, dissolved 31 January 2001.
  • Stated for each company that its records were not sent to The National Archives.
  • Stated for each company that all paper records and microfiche have been destroyed, and that the only records available are those captured on image, already listed in an earlier reply.
  • Explained that the Companies Act 2006, as amended by the Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023, allows Companies House to remove records of companies dissolved more than 20 years ago from public inspection, and that it has applied this.

What they did not do

  • Did not give a date for the destruction of either file, or name the retention schedule or authority under which it took place.
  • Did not state whether a transfer or destruction record is held for either file.
  • Did not answer whether a preservation hold is in place, a request first made on 10 October 2026.
  • Did not say whether the request was passed to its FOI team or give an FOI reference, as the requester asked.
  • Did not address the questions in the requester's emails of 29 and 31 October 2026 about the 1926 and 1938 conveyances.

Fair note

  • Companies House is a public authority under FOIA. Under section 8 any written request that gives a name and describes the information sought is a valid request, whichever team receives it, so the five held or not held questions fall within FOIA; an archive search team answering in service style is common practice and not a refusal in itself.
  • The earlier Companies House reply of 2 November 2026 set out a general disposal programme: paper files of England and Wales companies dissolved between 1992 and 2003 transferred to The National Archives or destroyed from July 2024, and microfiche disposed of or transferred by early 2024. Both companies were dissolved inside that window, so on Companies House's own dates the disposal came before the first preservation request of 10 October 2026. The replies are consistent with that programme.
  • The 20-year rule cited concerns public inspection of the register. It explains why records are no longer open to view; it is a different matter from the physical destruction of paper and microfiche, which is a records-management decision. The replies run the two together.
  • Conveyances of land are not generally among the documents filed at Companies House, so silence on the 1926 and 1938 conveyances may reflect that they were never held there; the replies do not say so.
  • Tone and speed were good: the team searched, answered per company and offered further help.

Open questions

  • On what date was each file destroyed, under which retention schedule or authority, and is a destruction or transfer record held?
  • Is any index entry or register extract still held for either company beyond the imaged filings?
  • Was a preservation hold considered after the requests of 10, 29 and 31 October 2026, or had disposal already taken place?
  • Has the request been logged under FOIA, and if so under what reference?
