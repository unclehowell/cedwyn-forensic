4 November 2026Journal entrySubject access response issued (five days after the recorded date)ref South Wales Police SAR 1293/26

Two emails of 4 November 2026 from the South Wales Police Data Protection Team (SAR 1293/26): at 14:43 UTC a response to the subject access request as a password-protected PDF portfolio, and three seconds later the password in a separate email. The request, sent on 28 September 2026 with a crime-recording request and a search note on FOI 719/26, was followed by clarification exchanges on 29 and 30 September and a chase on 21 October 2026.

Assessment (our reading): The response has been issued, slightly after the recorded one-month date and in a secure form. Its contents are personal and are not journalled; whether it answers every part of the request is the open point.

What they did

  • Issued a response to the subject access request as an encrypted PDF portfolio, with instructions for opening it on computers, phones and tablets.
  • Sent the password separately.
  • Responded on 4 November 2026, five days after 30 October 2026, the date recorded for the one-month period following the clarification exchange.

What they did not do

  • The contents are personal data and are not described here. Whether the response covers the crime-recording and search-note parts of the 28 September email is not checked from the cover email.
  • The cover email does not say whether any information was withheld, or under which Data Protection Act 2018 exemption.

Fair note

  • UK GDPR Article 12(3) allows one month, extendable by two further months for complex or numerous requests if the requester is told within the first month. A response a few days past the month is a minor delay, and police forces handle high volumes of subject access requests.
  • Delivering special-category personal data encrypted, with the password in a separate message, is good practice.

Open questions

  • Does the response cover every part of the 28 September request?
  • Was any information withheld, and under which exemption?
