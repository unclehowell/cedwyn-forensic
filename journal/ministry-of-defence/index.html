9 October 2026Journal entryNo information in scope located (late entry)ref MoD Defence Business Services SAR DPR2026/46503

Letter of 9 October 2026 from the Ministry of Defence, Defence Business Services Disclosure Team (Civilian), answering subject access request DPR2026/46503, received 5 August 2026. Journalled late: the letter is held in the master catalogue but did not reach the correspondence manifest.

Assessment (our reading): A clear nil return with the review routes set out, issued after the one-month period. Its scope is Defence Business Services' own holdings.

What they did

  • Searched the Defence Business Services storage facility and all Defence Business Services systems and located no information in scope; no exemption was cited.
  • Gave the routes onward: the Disclosures Team, then an internal review by the MOD Information Rights Compliance Team, then the Information Commissioner under section 165 of the Data Protection Act 2018.
  • Apologised for the delay.

What they did not do

  • No search outside Defence Business Services holdings (for example army unit records, military aid to the civil authorities records or other service records) is described.
  • No reason for the delay is given beyond volume of work.

Fair note

  • UK GDPR Article 12(3) gives one month for a subject access response, extendable in some cases; this reply came about two months after receipt.
  • A nil return from a records-services unit is common where the subject held no service or employment record there.

Open questions

  • Would a subject access request to other MoD record holders return anything?
