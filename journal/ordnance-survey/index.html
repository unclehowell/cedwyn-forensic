6 November 2026Journal entryNot held; five map extracts given as adviceref Ordnance Survey FOI-26-46 (naming of Ty Mawr on 1950-1975 plans)

Email of 6 November 2026, 16:02 UTC, from the Ordnance Survey FOI team answering FOI-26-46 after the requester's clarification of 29 October 2026. The request asked for map revision notes, resurvey information, name sheets or name books for the names 'Ty Mawr', 'Great House' and 'Great House Farm' on the 1:2,500 and 1:10,560 plans published 1950 to 1975, any record of who supplied or authorised a name, and any disposal schedule if such records were destroyed.

Assessment (our reading): A prompt, cooperative answer: nothing held on who authorised the name, but five dated map extracts that fix the change from 'Ty-Mawr' to 'Ty Mawr Farm' between 1965 and 1971, and a pointer to the missing Object Name Book. The disposal question in part 4 is not answered beyond 'not held'.

What they did

  • Answered each of the four numbered parts: the information is not held.
  • Under section 16, sent five archive map extracts (1947, published 1950; 1965; 1971; 1977; 1989) and stated that the property is named 'Ty-Mawr' in 1947 and 'Ty Mawr Farm' from between 1965 and 1971, still so in 1989, with no other name at the location.
  • Stated that a name would only have been changed on clear evidence from a sign on the property or confirmation from the owner or occupier.
  • Stated that the Object Name Book for the area (ST1673) is not held by Ordnance Survey and is recorded at The National Archives as 'Missing at transfer'.

What they did not do

  • Part 4 asked, if records were destroyed, for the disposal schedule or policy and the date; the reply gives 'not held' without stating whether 1950-1975 revision records once existed or how they were disposed of.
  • No record of who supplied the 'Ty Mawr Farm' form between 1965 and 1971 is identified.

Fair note

  • The reply came within 20 working days of the 29 October clarification (section 10, with the clock running from the clarification under section 1(3)).
  • The map extracts and the National Archives pointer are useful advice and assistance beyond what the Act requires, supplied without charge.
  • The rule that names change only on a sign or owner or occupier confirmation is Ordnance Survey's stated general practice, not a record of this change.

Open questions

  • Which source supplied the change from 'Ty-Mawr' to 'Ty Mawr Farm' between 1965 and 1971?
  • What happened to the post-1942 name records for this sheet, and is there a disposal record?
