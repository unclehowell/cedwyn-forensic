6 November 2026Journal entryNot environmental information; in any case refused under reg 12(4)(a)ref Dŵr Cymru EIR (historic water supply records, nomenclature supplement of 29 Oct 2026)

Email of 6 November 2026, 16:55 UTC, from the Dŵr Cymru Retail Services Customer Care Team answering the requester's two emails of 29 October 2026, which supplied additional search terms (historical and descriptive names for the farm) for the request of 7 June 2026 about records of the disconnection or termination of the water supply to Great House Farm.

Assessment (our reading): A reasoned position on the scope of the Regulations, with a fallback that nothing is held because 1980s records are past retention. The fallback rests on a retention period that is not identified, no search is described, and the reply omits the review and appeal information a regulation 14 refusal notice carries.

What they did

  • Considered the additional search terms.
  • Stated that information on the disconnection of a specific property's supply is not environmental information within regulation 2(1) of the Environmental Information Regulations 2004, with reasons tied to regulation 2(1)(a) and (b).
  • Stated that in any event no historic records, indexes, work-order records or archived material are held, 1980s customer and property records being outside its retention periods, so the request would be refused under regulation 12(4)(a).
  • Stated that it knows of no other public authority that may hold the information.

What they did not do

  • No retention schedule, retention period or disposal record is cited.
  • No search (systems, archives, terms) is described.
  • The reply does not mention the right to make representations under regulation 11 or the Information Commissioner, which regulation 14(5) requires in a refusal notice.
  • The reply does not address regulation 2(1)(c) (measures and activities affecting the elements of the environment) when concluding the information falls outside regulation 2(1).

Fair note

  • Dŵr Cymru is a public authority for the Environmental Information Regulations only, not for the Freedom of Information Act, so if the information is not environmental no statutory access regime applies to it.
  • Whether property-level water-supply records are environmental information is a question on which the Information Commissioner's guidance takes a broad reading of 'measures'; the company's narrower reading is reasoned.
  • The reply to the 29 October supplement came within 20 working days (regulation 5(2)). The original request of 7 June 2026 received customer-service answers in September 2026, after the 20 working days had run.

Open questions

  • Which retention period applied to 1980s disconnection or work-order records, and is any disposal record held?
  • Will Dŵr Cymru treat a request for review as representations under regulation 11?
